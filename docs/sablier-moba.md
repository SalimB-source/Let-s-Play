# MOBA solo — implementation and remaining work

## Availability and scope

The board shares the classic 1.5-second turn banner: it starts after the mulligan, changes only when the active camp changes, and stays silent at match end. The active camp remains marked during defensive response windows; the separate instruction identifies who may respond. The banner is non-interactive and announced politely to assistive technology.

When the AI announces a creature, spell or audited weapon that the human may answer, the MOBA shows the classic card preview with its lane/target and **Répondre** / **Laisser passer**. Responding closes only the preview; the card stays pending, can be inspected again and can still be passed after a legal response. AI responses to human announcements remain automatic. Existing response-window restrictions and Embuscade suspension rules are unchanged.

The disabled online entry in `RpgDuelPage.jsx` opens an **experimental, local solo** MOBA match against an AI. If online mode is enabled, its existing link is unchanged. The MOBA match has no online referee, persistence or resume. It is playable with five audited, validated 30-card preconstructed decks, or player-built decks using only the current 220-card pool (including Vampires, Poison creatures and Nécropole reinforcements). It is **not** full-catalogue MOBA: unaudited cards, many card effects, and some response windows are not supported. These limits must not be removed without auditing the engine paths below.

Before the match, the player can choose a saved player-built deck or one of the five MOBA preconstructed decks, or customize a local draft. Saved decks that do not pass the 30-card, 1–3 colour, copy-limit and MOBA card-audit rules are shown disabled with their reason; cards are never silently replaced. The opponent receives an unmodified random deck from the currently compatible Tower floors (23 out of 49 at this update). The remaining Tower decks contain unaudited cards and must not be enabled until their mechanics are adapted and tested. Both decks are validated before the match. The AI chooses its mana, discards, summons, targets, attacks, spells and blocks automatically; its hand remains private. The AI evaluates spells and up to 31 attack sets on isolated copies. With no fatigue an empty-deck game can still reach a stalemate; the AI favours incremental damage in that situation, without guaranteeing an end to every game.

## Classic table elements

Both camps show the classic deck and graveyard piles, and the ten-slot mana tracks show their real acquired and available mana (including a cost preview for the selected card). The human chooses the new permanent mana at the start of each eligible turn with the classic element chooser (including its acquired-source counts and 1–3 keyboard shortcuts); a single-colour deck chooses automatically and the AI chooses its own colour. Existing sources refill at the start of their owner’s turn, payment dims them, and no new source is granted after the ten-source cap. Inspecting a hand card also previews its cost or missing mana without playing it. The chooser remains accessible without scrolling the table. The AI deck and hand remain face down; only the public top graveyard card is shown. Nonempty graveyards open the original filtered card viewer, with keyboard focus contained and restored on close. The local AI pauses while it is open. The sound toggle, classic separate sliders for effects, character voices and music, and (when available) fullscreen control are accessible from the table. The sliders share the classic game preferences; the local AI pauses while audio settings are open. The engine’s arrival-audio journals now play available character voices and the existing generic pirate, ship and dwarf greetings, including supported Tenacité returns, without exposing or preloading the AI hand. Other audio and visual effects are not yet at full classic parity. Mobile positions are compact so the lanes and table itself remain fixed; auxiliary viewers may scroll. This brings back visible elements of the classic interface, not all its animations, settings, mechanics or the unaudited cards.

The classic full-card preview opens on right-click or touch/pen long-press for visible cards in the mulligan, hand, board and player deck search. Inspection buttons also provide a keyboard-accessible route; hovering alone does not open a preview. The gesture consumes its subsequent click, so inspection never plays a card. The modal pauses the local AI, restores focus on close and retains the creature's displayed Furie and equipped effects. Played cards share the classic table's proportional element, statistic and effect-marker sizing; hand markers shrink with their card so they fit the fixed board.


During combat, the original red attack and yellow block arrows are reused. A direct attack points to the opposing HP marker in its own lane, a targeted attack points to its selected same-lane creature, and a blocked attack is replaced by the assigned blocker arrows. A live text equivalent identifies the creatures and lane. These are indications of declared choices, not predictions of combat damage; they do not alter the rules.


## Rules and engine integration

* `rpgMoba.js` owns serializable lanes: 2/1/2 slots per side, independent 20 / 10 / 20 HP values, assignments, active/conquered status and a winner after two conquests. Damage and healing are clamped; a conquered lane cannot be used again. Side-lane conquest removes both sides' occupants via the engine's exile cleanup. On the first central conquest, only the loser's central occupants are exiled: the winner must choose an active flank, and their surviving central creature keeps its central slot but attacks, blocks, and uses lane effects as part of that flank. If the chosen flank falls, its attached central creature is removed too. The second conquest ends the match and stops further damage. The board uses a three-corridor map (including a walkable center path). After the central choice, a highlighted band joins only the winner's half of center to their chosen flank, the divider disappears on that half, and the center's winning side displays no separate HP gauge: the two existing friendly HP reserves are added to the chosen flank (up to 30 HP), without resetting prior damage. Healing on that merged flank also caps at 30 HP. The opponent's lanes and HP remain separate. The original engine owns payment, attack readiness, combat modifiers, creature HP and spell effects.
* `rpgMobaLocal.js` uses an existing Mana Essai duel and maps its five slots to lanes. The engine's `pvp` flag selects manual control, not networking. It rejects occupied or inactive slots instead of allowing the classic full-board sacrifice and checks lane/engine placement agreement before game actions. Summons are rejected before mutation unless the creature belongs to the audited combat pool; this prevents an unsupported creature from entering play and deadlocking later combat.
* Creature blocks must be in the attacker's lane. An authoritative declaration applies banners once, then hands control to the defender. The defender can play audited instants before assigning blocks. Destroyed attackers leave the declaration; a surviving frozen attacker resolves under classic declared-combat rules. End of turn also opens a defender instant-response window, and only an explicit pass starts the next turn. The Lionne’s, Maître’s, Vipère’s, Sonneur’s and Corbeau’s creature-targeted arrivals additionally open the existing engine response window when the defender has a playable instant. A targeted-spell window is exposed when Poigne de givre, Ressac, Éclair du marteau, Trait de braise, Appât de la meute, Frappe d’iaijutsu, Courant de retour, Haut-fond, Lame de mer, Effacement des faibles, Le Jugement des colosses, Feux de kitsune, Défi du Jarl or Brise-lame is cast at an opposing creature during the active player’s main phase. Éclair du marteau and Trait de braise also open the window when aimed at the opposing HP in an active lane, Eau de la source and Festin des vaillants open it when aimed at a friendly active lane, and Le Poids and La Grande Pesée open it for their selected transfer lane. Rapport du greffier, Le Routier, Papyrus, Lettre de marque and Les feuillets open it for their respective decks, without revealing the cards there; Nom effacé announces the opposing hand without revealing its contents. Tempête de sable, Vent rouge, Brisure de verre, Brume du fjord, Hurlement du blizzard, Verglas, Nuage de cendres and Embuscade announce the opposing camp’s creatures without targeting an individual. Le Grand Hiver, Tempête de foudre et de givre, La Dernière Pesée, Ressac de la lune noire and Neige noire instead announce creatures of both camps. Mur de boucliers, Serment de la forge, Rempart de dunes, Offrande de lin and Le Grand Débarquement announce the caster’s allied creatures across lanes. Grand Voile blanc announces its combat-wide effect for both camps; Réanimation announces the graveyard and selected allied slot. Parade de verre, Voile de la lune noire, Sabre maudit and Mémoire de l’armurerie also open this window when targeting an ally, as do the two neutral destruction spells when targeting either camp. The announcement identifies the relevant creature, lane, deck or group of creatures and its camp. The card and mana, as well as La Grande Pesée’s, Offrande de lin’s, Neige noire’s and Papyrus’s selected HP prices, are paid at announcement; the other side can play audited fast spells and explicitly pass. Resolution rechecks the target and usually fizzles if it has left or become illegal; a lane conquered in response is no longer legal and the spell is not redirected. Festin is an exception: if its selected lane is conquered, its independent global healing still applies to surviving allied creatures, but no lane is healed. A winning response ends the match and consumes the pending card. Fast spells played as responses resolve immediately without opening nested windows. Silence d’écume and Déflection can cancel the single pending opposing spell; they cannot counter a response, a creature or an ability. The announced spell and its costs are not paid again, and Déflection draws with its Link evaluated at the counter. This is deliberately not a general spell stack. The 48 main-phase spells in the current audited pool can announce in their supported form; other card abilities and unaudited effects are not covered by this spell window. The AI evaluates defensive responses against a simulated resolved threat without consuming the live RNG. The local board opts into this window; classic duel rules and direct adapter callers without the opt-in remain unchanged.
* Creature announcement: both sides now announce a creature and its chosen lane/slot after paying and removing it from hand, before it enters. Écueil counters an opposing creature; Contresort des abîmes counters either an opposing creature or spell, while Silence d’écume and Déflection remain spell-only. No counter can target a response or a creature ability. Passing places the creature without paying again; if the lane or slot is no longer legal, the paid creature goes to its graveyard. A targeted arrival choice and any applicable response to its ability are subsequent, separate steps, not nested windows. A winning response ends the game and clears the reservation. The AI evaluates the creature threat on isolated copies; the board opts in, while direct adapter calls and classic duels retain their existing behavior.
* Audited spells include the global trial spells, Poigne de givre and Ressac against an opposing creature, and Éclair du marteau / Trait de braise against a creature or selected active opposing lane. Ressac returns a creature to its owner’s hand and reconciles the lane and any declared attack. Parade de verre targets an allied creature and blocks its next hostile spell or effect according to the classic engine. Rempart de dunes grants its printed temporary defence bonus to allied creatures across active lanes. Papyrus pays its printed two-HP cost from a chosen friendly active lane with more than two HP, then draws using the original engine; it can also be played in supported response windows. Festin des vaillants heals all allied creatures and one chosen friendly lane; Eau de la source heals a chosen friendly lane. The Porteuse de cruches heals its own lane on arrival, including an audited scheduled return. The engine retains the card text and costs. Explicitly global creature effects remain global across active lanes.
* Valkyrie des glaces is available in custom decks: her arrival damages other creatures in both camps across all lanes and independently rolls the printed freeze chance. Friendly shields are not consumed, hostile shields block the effect, and her own shield and Envol remain intact. Tenacité repeats the wave in the original lane. The AI probes the wave and avoids it when it loses more position value than it gains; it uses a fixed probe RNG, not a full probability search.
* Braisier du souk deals its printed one damage to enemy creatures across all lanes, then hits only the opposing HP of its own original lane. Shields and death triggers still apply; conquest exiles survivors without death effects, and victory stops later effects. The effect also runs on a Tenacité return to that lane. The AI probes a winning arrival before choosing a more expensive summon. It is available in custom decks; presets are unchanged.
* The five rapid ephemeral Braise creatures (Ifrit furtif and four Djinns) use printed Rapidité, Overkill, Envol and Infiltration where applicable, attack their own lane and are destroyed at end of turn. The engine journal and lane slots are reconciled after the transition. New audited spells include Tempête de sable, Vent rouge and Brisure de verre (hostile creatures across lanes), La Dernière Pesée (all creatures), Ressac de la lune noire (global return excluding Vampires), Rapport du greffier (draw), Les feuillets (opponent deck), Frappe d’iaijutsu, Courant de retour, Haut-fond, Lame de mer and Voile de la lune noire. The latter five require their printed friendly/enemy targets; the exiling Lame does not trigger Anqa’s death return. Classic instant timing, protection and shields still apply. The original five preset decks are unchanged; additions are available in the editor.
* The Veilleuse des citernes shields another chosen allied creature across lanes; with no legal ally, its existing engine fallback shields itself. Djinn de la flamme mourante draws on arrival, can attack immediately and expires at end of turn; Scalde des runes draws, while Valkyrie au corbeau draws only a Viking. Scribe des feuillets mills one enemy card on death, and Drakkar à tête de dragon cannot attack but draws on death. Conquest exiles without firing death effects. All six are available in the editor; starter decks are unchanged.
* Guerrier au bouclier rond enters its chosen lane and then requires its controller to select an allied creature (or itself) for its printed shield. Other actions are blocked while this target is pending; scheduled returns use the classic automatic arrival targeting.
* La Lionne du midi ardent targets an opposing creature when it arrives. If an instant response is available, the defender acts privately before the printed damage resolves; a removed target causes the effect to fizzle. Without a legal target, its arrival has no target.
* Le Maître du marteau can strike an opposing creature or its own opposing lane on arrival; the engine supplies its printed Furie, Overkill, Envol and Tenacité. A scheduled Tenacité return retains the original lane and routes an automatically selected sorcerer hit to that lane. If the return wins the game, later returns and turn-start effects are stopped.
* Sahir des braises draws on arrival and deals its printed pre-combat damage to the creature it blocks or that blocks it; it cannot strike a creature in another lane. Scheduled Anqa and Tenacité returns retain their originating lane, wait for a free slot there and are cancelled when that lane is conquered or unknown. The real Anqa global return effect is supported and board removals are reconciled after transitions. Other creation, exile and revival paths are not generally supported.
* By player decision, an empty deck produces no fatigue in MOBA. The agreed rule is: a creature effect targeting a sorcerer maps to the source creature's lane; a spell or HP cost without a creature source lets the player select an active lane; explicitly global effects remain global. This is implemented for the audited healing and spell-damage effects above; the Maître’s creature-source wizard damage and Papyrus’s HP cost are also routed; other HP-cost cards and most other wizard effects still require adaptation and remain unavailable. Classic fatigue and classic sorcerer HP/victory logic remain unchanged. MOBA victory is determined by lanes, not the engine's legacy wizard HP.

* La Dévoreuse exiles an opposing creature; Valkyrie des neuf nuits and Père des corbeaux exile an eligible creature from either camp under their printed cost threshold. Opponents can respond to hostile targets; allied targets resolve without an opposing response. Tenacité returns Père to his original lane and automatically repeats the arrival, as with the existing scheduled-return rule. Kraken can destroy an opposing Navire, with its normal response and death triggers.
* Appât de la meute and Embuscade mark blockers; each marked creature must block if a legal attacker is in its own lane. Attacks already targeted at creatures cannot be blocked, and the AI assigns forced blockers within their lanes. Temporary stat changes now remember their actual clamped deltas, so the 1/1 minimum does not turn into an unintended buff when they expire.
* Gardien de la porte ardente provides same-lane Défenseur priority, Provocation and targeted-attack retaliation. Rempart des sables éternels shields allies across lanes; Tin Hinan grants temporary Infiltration to other allies; Serpent du monde replaces both hands; Ifrit al-Ahmar grants global Djinn resonance; Serment de la forge grants temporary targeted attacks to allies.
* Yamina heals allied creatures globally and the friendly HP in her own lane; Dame des neuf mondes heals her own lane and has same-lane Attaque ciblée. Their creature-source healing never changes the legacy wizard HP.
* Chacal du seuil, Veilleuse au corbeau noir, Berserk du crépuscule, Seigneur des mâchoires, Faucon du zénith and Celui qui juge use Tribut after real damage to their own opposing lane; that lane's friendly HP is restored up to 10 while the opposing HP is reduced. A transfer can conquer a lane, but it cannot heal an already closed lane. Offrande de lin pays HP from a chosen friendly lane and grants temporary Tribut to allies across lanes. Prêtre de la bouche transfers on arrival, Ouvrier du natron transfers on death and Gardienne de la balance heals on death in their respective source lanes. Le Poids lets its caster select a lane for both sides of the transfer; La Grande Pesée separately selects a payment lane and transfer lane, honors La Dévoreuse's Lien, and draws unless that transfer wins immediately. Classic wizard HP and shields are not used in these MOBA transfers.
* Amani and Kenshi give their adjacency bonuses only to neighboring slots in the same lane: the touching edges of different lanes are not adjacent. Samurai resonance remains explicitly global. Rōnin du Soleil rouge, Hatamoto des cendres, Cavalier des deux sabres, Shogun promu, Onna-musha des cendres, Tengu du mont ardent, Kenshi and Susanoo keep their printed Harakiri: on death it chooses a living opposing creature across lanes, or strikes the opposing HP in the source's lane if no enemy creature exists. Shields and protection apply; conquest exiles without death effects, and a second conquest stops further triggers. Susanoo can exile either side's eligible creature costing five or more, with the normal hostile response window, and its Nuage de cendres synergy remains active.
* Le Maître des corbeaux uses the same neutral, cost-limited exile choice and hostile response window as Père des corbeaux, has lane-based Tribut, and returns to its original lane with Tenacité and an automatically chosen arrival target. Guetteur du beffroi counts its controller's turns; its final strike damages its own opposing lane, then draws unless the strike wins the match. Conquest removes its counters and prevents exiled neighbors from ringing. Its counters are visible on the MOBA board. Faussoyeur des dunes recycles the latest eligible graveyard creature on arrival and uses lane-based Butin; Dune marchante and Scribe de la Liste recover the latest eligible card on death (matching the classic engine's automatic recycling), but not when exiled by conquest.
* Tarek and Daimyō create dogs and samurai on arrival; Momie de lin and Orochi create Shabti and Petit Orochi on death. Tokens use the first free slot in their source's lane, then free slots in other active lanes in left-to-right order; any excess cannot enter. Simultaneous victims free their slots before their death triggers resolve. Tokens obey normal lane combat and permanent assignments, cannot be added to decks, and cease to exist rather than joining the MOBA graveyard on death or conquest. Tarek's aura remains lane-adjacent, Daimyō's banner applies to attacking samurai across lanes, and conquest causes no death triggers. This deterministic placement rule covers these audited tokens only.
* A creature's lane slot is reserved before its arrival resolves, allowing a summoned creature to die to chained death effects without rolling back a valid play.
* Fennec des décombres, Chien du guet and Le Renégat apply Butin only after real damage to their own opposing lane. A non-winning conquest still triggers the conquering attacker's Butin even though conquest exiles it; attacks skipped after that exile, prevented damage and attackers killed in ordinary combat do not trigger. The second conquest ends the game immediately, before further Butin choices. Multiple human loot discards are queued, while the AI discards automatically; an empty deck has no fatigue.
* Tiziri lets its controller select an eligible Magie Rapide or Sorcellerie from their deck; Pilote de la Carreira selects a Navire. The private human choice blocks other actions and the AI resolves its own search without rendering or logging its card. If no matching card exists, arrival finishes without a choice. Classic search behavior remains unchanged.
* Nom effacé and Celle qui pèse force the defender to discard; Ibis des comptes, Amayas, Jörmungandr and Le Routier draw then discard. When a human choice is required, the AI pauses even during its own turn and does not reveal its hand; the adapter blocks other actions until the discard is chosen.
* Attaque ciblée can target opponents in the attacker’s lane; a centre-lane attacker can additionally target creatures in either active side lane, for both players and the AI. Défenseur priority and the classic protection and miss rules still apply to the eligible targets. If no eligible enemy creature exists, the attacker may hit its own lane’s HP; a target lost after declaration cancels the strike rather than redirecting it. Ordinary attacks and blocks remain in the attacker’s lane. The ten new targeted attackers include cards with secondary abilities, which still need broader MOBA-specific coverage.
* Völva soigneuse heals an allied creature (including herself), Faiseur de passage grants another ally temporary Infiltration, Vipère de verre debuffs an enemy, Sonneur fêlé returns an enemy and Corbeau de givre freezes an enemy costing at most three. The defender can respond to these hostile arrivals. Géant des banquises deals damage and freezes enemies; Léviathan returns other creatures across all lanes. Lane occupancy is reconciled after global arrivals.
* Effacement des faibles and Le Jugement des colosses target either side, enforcing the printed mana-cost threshold. Neige noire pays three HP from a chosen friendly lane before destroying cheap creatures and freezing survivors across the table. Nuage de cendres hits enemies across lanes and its echo resolves on the caster's next turn.

* Sabre maudit attaches to a friendly creature and supplies its printed bonuses, critical chance and Vol de vie. Vol de vie heals the source creature’s active lane (up to the lane’s maximum (20 on the sides, 10 in the centre)), never the unused wizard HP; a conquered lane cannot be healed. Mémoire de l’armurerie automatically reserves a Sabre from the caster’s graveyard, and rechecks both the bearer and the Sabre after a response. Brise-lame targets an armed enemy and, as in the classic engine, destroys **all** of its attached weapons before drawing. The board exposes equipment and Vol de vie, and the AI probes all three cards on isolated copies. Thirteen printed weapons are now audited; all other prototype weapons and unaudited equipment remain excluded (see the updates below).

* Lettre de marque draws and grants boarding to the next creature played that turn. The local board first asks for an eligible enemy prize, then handles a separate targeted arrival, with no nested response. No eligible prize leaves any valid arrival available; no remaining arrival target dissipates that part. Direct non-opt-in summons without a targeted arrival retain the classic automatic prize choice; already-deferred targeted arrivals use the same sequential choices. A captive frees its original slot and keeps its permanent lane assignment. On the captor’s death it returns without a new arrival to its original slot, or another free slot in that same active lane; if the lane is full or inactive it returns to its owner’s hand. On bounce, exile, conquest or a second capture, it returns to hand; a token that would enter hand ceases to exist. Captured equipment is discarded, auras are recalculated and temporary effects continue expiring while held. Le Grand Débarquement buffs only creatures still holding a prize when it resolves. Both Lettre’s Chasseur Link and Le Grand Débarquement’s Barbe-Rousse Link are supported and rechecked at resolution.

## Known limitations and risks

1. Most of the catalogue is unaudited: attacks, arrivals, deaths, auras, target selection, global effects, weapons, boarding, interception, tokens, HP costs, temporary exile and revival must all be checked before enabling arbitrary cards. Placement, combat and spell checks fail closed for unsupported creatures. The placement rollback for unexpected engine mutations restores serialized duel state, but not engine-side ephemeral journals or RNG; it is not a general transaction.
2. Only declaration, end-of-turn, creature announcements, the thirteen audited weapon attachments, audited hostile creature-targeted arrivals, and the forty-eight main-phase spells described above have response windows. Six of these allow a response when targeting a lane; the two shield spells and two neutral destruction spells allow a response when targeting an allied creature. The classic engine's other fast-response, target and stack interactions need explicit lane-aware support. Some ability targeting choices and presentation also remain incomplete.
   All 52 spells in `RPG_MAGIE_CARDS` are in the audited pool. Tour Oubliée cards are separately outside the normal duel catalogue and remain unavailable, as do unaudited creatures and printed weapons except the thirteen listed in the updates below. Printed boarding creatures and equipment are not automatically enabled by the two spell adaptations.
3. A headless Chromium visual smoke test at desktop and mobile viewport sizes uses a controlled board fixture; real-device complete matches, broader browser compatibility and accessibility testing are still necessary. There is no save/resume or online MOBA; online integration would additionally require authoritative lane validation, filtered views and concurrency/reconnect tests.
4. Further testing must cover the full catalogue, all entry/exit paths, forced/targeted combat, ongoing effects, post-victory actions and real-device complete matches. Do not describe this trial as the complete requested mode until those paths are implemented and verified.

## Files

* `src/games/rpgMoba.js` — lane bookkeeping, price payment and validation.
* `src/games/rpgMobaAI.js` — lane-aware heuristic decisions; probes use isolated copies and a separate deterministic RNG, while real moves use the adapter. It compares legal audited spells, simulates at most 31 attack sets and assigns blocks per lane using its own HP. It avoids gratuitous board wipes, late temporary buffs, and ephemeral summons after combat. It cannot predict hidden responses, and ignores milling as a win condition because MOBA has no fatigue.
* `src/games/rpgMobaLocal.js` — local adapter, audited deck validation and match creation, starter decks, turns, responses, audited spells, summons and combat.
* `src/games/rpgDuel.js` — optional lane damage/healing hooks, lane-aware blocking, conquest cleanup, scheduled returns, no-fatigue and lane victory handling. Ordinary classic calls retain their existing path.
* `src/games/RpgDuelPage.jsx` — local MOBA menu entry and shared classic card/mulligan/inspection and spell-announcement components; optional table-card props leave classic defaults unchanged. Online link remains intact.
* `src/games/RpgMobaBoard.jsx`, `src/games/RpgMobaDeckEditor.jsx`, `src/games/rpg-moba.css` — solo board, audited deck editor, automated AI turns and lane choices. The production board reuses the classic duel’s full-art table and mulligan cards, effect icons, cost/element/stat markers, card preview, compact spell announcement, card entry, surviving combat and spell-hit animations, and the original death ghosts (sand or Braise flames) on the affected slots; each seat’s terrain comes from the original deck-to-board resolver. The table uses the same full-screen terrain paintings and card faces as the classic duel, with two vertical separators for its three lanes. Lane HP and conquest UI remain MOBA-specific. The battlefield and main game screen do not scroll; hands larger than six cards and the latest twelve journal entries use page buttons without changing card indices. Lane healing and blocked shields get feedback; the original SFX player handles selected spell, draw, healing and combat sounds and the existing mute preference, with a local sound toggle. Spell notifications capture the target before resolution. Animation settings are inherited from the classic game. Death ghosts are driven by the engine destruction journal, not returns to hand or conquest exiles; they never reserve a slot and are omitted in reduced motion while death sounds remain. Not all classic spell, death or audio effects are wired.
* `tests/rpg-moba.test.js`, `tests/rpg-moba-local.test.js`, `tests/rpg-moba-board.test.js`, `tests/rpg-mode-cards.test.js` — lane, adapter, jsdom board and menu coverage.

### Proposition d'implémentation des créatures Embuscade en MOBA

Les dix créatures avec `reactionTourAdverse` restent exclues du pool : il ne suffit pas de satisfaire `rpgConditionEmbuscadeRemplie` pour les jouer. Pour une première tranche, le Garde du trône givré est le candidat le plus simple : son déclencheur est la déclaration d'attaque et il n'a pas d'effet d'arrivée imprimé. Même lui peut recevoir un Abordage de la prochaine arrivée, qui demanderait une cible ; cet état doit être pris en charge ou explicitement refusé avant toute dépense.

La solution proposée est de suspendre la fenêtre courante pendant l'annonce de l'Embuscade, puis de la reprendre après sa résolution ou son contre. Le contexte suspendu doit conserver le combat, les choix éventuels et le camp ayant la priorité ; le propriétaire d'une annonce n'est plus nécessairement `duel.tour`. Il faut :

1. Valider le déclencheur, la main, le mana, les sacrifices et une case légale avant paiement ; permettre aussi l'invocation ordinaire pendant le tour du propriétaire.
2. Annoncer la créature et donner à son adversaire une vraie réponse avant son arrivée, y compris Écueil et Contresort des abîmes. Ne jamais passer par le contre automatique propre au duel classique. Sur contre, payer chaque carte une seule fois, placer les cartes au cimetière et reprendre la fenêtre initiale. Sur passage, placer la créature puis traiter ses effets et choix d'arrivée avant la reprise. Si une voie ou une case a changé, appliquer une résolution explicite et cohérente sans duplications ni remboursement implicite.
3. Adapter ensemble l'admission au catalogue, `rpgMobaJouerCreature`, `rpgMobaPasserCreature`, `rpgMobaLancerMagie`, les conditions de réponse et la résolution des effets. La réponse à une annonce hors tour ne peut pas dépendre uniquement de `side !== duel.tour` ; les annonces et leurs contre-effets ne doivent pas effacer le combat ou sa fenêtre suspendue.
4. Dans le plateau, calculer le camp qui agit d'après la fenêtre effective plutôt que toujours d'après le défenseur du tour ; offrir la sélection de main, la voie, la case et les sacrifices à ce camp, puis les réponses et la reprise du blocage. Adapter également l'IA pour jouer, contrer ou passer, en simulant sur copie sans consommer le hasard réel.
5. Tester les deux camps : conditions vraies et fausses, mana et places insuffisants, changements de voie, absence de mutation sur refus, paiement unique, contre et passage, conservation des attaquants, blocage dans la même voie, changements pendant la réponse, IA, interface et absence de régression du duel classique.

Après le Garde, chaque carte demande un audit distinct : Sentinelle (dégâts à un attaquant), Éclipse (affaiblissement), Gardienne (cible alliée et bouclier), Traqueur (deux attaquants et renvoi/destruction), Veilleuse (gel), Sanguinaire (dégâts de zone), Porte-étendard (Vampire ciblé ou bloqueur), Tojiro (Djinn adverse, sacrifice et effets de combat/mort) et Sorcier du ressac noir (contre d'une créature jouée depuis la main). En particulier, la condition de blocage du Porte-étendard nécessite une fenêtre après la désignation des bloqueurs, et le Sorcier doit contrer une annonce MOBA déjà payée plutôt qu'une carte encore en main. Ne pas étendre le pool tant que chaque chemin n'est pas couvert ; Boualem, également exclu, n'est pas une créature Embuscade et doit être audité séparément.

La voie de contre automatique du duel classique refuse désormais explicitement les duels MOBA, même si leur drapeau `pvp` était absent. Les nouveaux tests vérifient ce garde-fou, les contres explicites MOBA des deux camps, le maintien du contre classique et le refus sans mutation du Garde malgré une attaque déclarée. **Cela ne rend pas encore les Embuscades jouables en MOBA.**

Combat, annonces de créature, sorts, capacités et fin de tour disposent des fenêtres adaptées. L'IA choisit et simule son sacrifice sur une copie de la partie.

Esquive et Contre-attaque restent celles de la carte : la riposte à un sort frappe la voie adverse correspondant à Tojiro, et celle à une créature frappe sa source. Comme les autres Harakiri déjà documentés en MOBA, le sien choisit une créature adverse à travers les voies ; à défaut, il frappe la voie adverse de Tojiro. Les Sites ne sont pas des créatures éligibles. Une Réanimation n'est pas une invocation depuis la main et ne demande ni condition d'Embuscade ni sacrifice.

Les dix créatures portant Embuscade sont désormais dans le pool audité. Boualem reste exclu séparément et n'est pas une carte Embuscade.

### Choix des sacrifices par l’IA

En duel classique comme en MOBA, l’IA examine les combinaisons légales de victimes (une ou deux), en privilégiant les moins précieuses. Elle compare leur force et leur défense actuelles ainsi que leur valeur tactique — notamment capacités, protections, équipement et caractère éphémère — à celles de la nouvelle créature. Un remplacement défavorable n’est envisagé que si une simulation isolée révèle un avantage concret : arrivée profitable, victoire au combat ou prévention d’une défaite au prochain combat. En MOBA les placements et blocages sont évalués par voie ; Tojiro applique aussi cette prudence en Embuscade. Les simulations sont bornées et utilisent leur propre hasard : elles restent des estimations, sans connaissance des futures actions adverses. En classique, le choix est réévalué après la fenêtre de réponse si le plateau a changé.

## Refonte : attaques individuelles

Le plateau local, l'IA et l'adaptateur utilisent les attaques individuelles. Les anciennes signatures MOBA refusent les groupes et les blocages. Le moteur réutilise en interne `phase = blocage` pour la fenêtre de réponse, sans permettre de blocage. Les sections historiques ci-dessus décrivent l'ancien fonctionnement lorsqu'elles mentionnent des blocages : les présentes règles prévalent.

Sur le plateau, sélectionner une cible ou choisir la frappe directe annonce aussitôt l'attaque ; si la frappe directe est la seule possibilité, sélectionner l'attaquant suffit. Un choix de cible peut être annulé avant l'annonce. Seul le défenseur peut répondre ou passer : lorsque l'IA défend, le joueur attaquant ne peut pas passer à sa place.

Règles décidées pour l'intégration : chaque créature peut attaquer une fois par
tour ; après l'annonce, une fenêtre Magie Rapide/Embuscade précède les dégâts.
L'attaque normale vise une créature accessible de la même voie avec échange
simultané, ou directement la voie si aucune n'est accessible. Sans Envol on ne
vise pas une créature volante ; avec Envol, si aucun ennemi volant accessible
n'existe, on peut choisir une créature au sol ou la voie. Infiltration protège
contre les attaques normales. L'Attaque ciblée peut viser une créature sur tout
le plateau, y compris Envol et Infiltration, ou directement la voie ; elle ne
reçoit pas de riposte sauf Contre-attaque. Les protections et échecs existants
restent applicables.

Parmi les cibles accessibles, Provocation prime sur Défenseur ; à défaut,
Défenseur ne s’impose qu’aux attaques venant de sa propre voie de combat. Une
priorité applicable interdit la frappe directe ; une créature avec Provocation
gagne +0/+1 par attaque qui la cible, cumulable jusqu'à la fin du tour. Une cible est fixée à l'annonce : si elle
disparaît ou devient illégale, la frappe est annulée sans redirection ; un
nouveau Défenseur n'est pas rétroactif. Overkill inflige à la voie les dégâts
dépassant la défense de la créature attaquée, uniquement si le coup porte.

Déjà appliqué dans l'adaptateur MOBA : Appât de la meute et le sort Embuscade,
qui imposent des blocages, sont injouables (et exclus des choix de l'IA). Le
Traqueur des nuits sans lune ne peut plus utiliser son Embuscade à deux
attaquants, mais conserve son effet normal d'arrivée. Le duel classique n'est
pas modifié. Certains anciens tests MOBA décrivant ces cartes comme jouables
par blocage devront être remplacés lors de la refonte, pas pris pour des tests
du nouveau parcours.

Les effets exigeant un blocage ou plusieurs attaquants ne se déclenchent pas : notamment Vampire des braises, Sahir, Cavalier de vapeur et les Tours. Le Porte-étendard ne réagit plus à un blocage mais conserve sa réaction à une Attaque ciblée de Vampire. Le gel de combat conserve sa règle existante de déclenchement avant les blessures. Les aperçus signalent les adaptations MOBA sans altérer le catalogue classique. Les anciens tests qui exigent un blocage restent à réécrire ; les scénarios de réponses imbriquées, les effets de toutes les cartes et le PvP en ligne ne sont pas exhaustivement validés.

### Quotas de cartes par tour

Chaque camp dispose d'une invocation normale de créature par tour, quel que soit son coût, et d'une Embuscade supplémentaire pendant le tour adverse. Chaque camp peut aussi jouer un sort et une arme par tour (dont le Sabre maudit). Une annonce légale consomme son quota même si elle est contrée ; la résolution ne le consomme pas une seconde fois. Tojiro en Embuscade ne demande pas de sacrifice si un Djinn adverse est présent à l'annonce ; sa pose normale conserve le coût habituel. Les quotas des deux camps sont réinitialisés au changement de tour. Les Sites, jetons et réanimations ne sont pas des invocations depuis la main. Ces quotas ne créent pas de nouvelles fenêtres de réaction ; les règles des autres modes restent inchangées.

Le centre commence avec 15 PV par camp. La refonte après conquête est en cours : suppression du dialogue de rattachement et du reliquat de PV, présence de la créature centrale sur les deux flancs actifs et choix de la voie pour les attaques. Les soins d’arrivée et plusieurs interactions spatiales ont été adaptés. Les autres effets, priorités, cas de victoire et suites historiques restent à auditer et tester : cette version est un travail en cours, non validé pour fusion.

Depuis cette ébauche, les transferts d'arrivée et de mort, la Contre-attaque,
le retour par Ténacité, la Réanimation, les jetons et les flèches de combat
ont été adaptés au centre à deux voies. Les essais couvrent notamment les deux
camps, les deux flancs, une zone sans double impact, un flanc perdu et une
victoire par capacité centrale. Les anciennes suites Garde/Tojiro et les suites
ciblées de conquête/sacrifice ont été réconciliées avec les nouvelles règles.

La validation globale reste ouverte : sur une sélection historique de 968 tests
MOBA (moteur local, IA et effets de voie), 763 échouent encore ; les mêmes noms
échouaient au précédent point de contrôle de cette PR. Cette comparaison ne
prouve pas que chaque échec a la même cause et ne remplace pas la révision des
scénarios historiques ni une comparaison avec `main`. La PR reste en brouillon.
