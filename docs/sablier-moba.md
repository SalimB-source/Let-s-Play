# MOBA solo — implementation and remaining work

## Availability and scope

The disabled online entry in `RpgDuelPage.jsx` opens an **experimental, local solo** MOBA match against an AI. If online mode is enabled, its existing link is unchanged. The MOBA match has no online referee, persistence or resume. It is playable with five audited, validated 30-card preconstructed decks, or player-built decks using only the current 129-card pool (including newly enabled targeted attackers and discard effects). It is **not** full-catalogue MOBA: unaudited cards, many card effects, and some response windows are not supported. These limits must not be removed without auditing the engine paths below.

The player chooses both decks (including the opponent’s): **Sable et givre** (snow, sand, embers), **Sources et givre** (snow, water, embers), **Le cercle des braises** (snow, sand, embers), **La source ardente** (snow, water, embers), or **Les serments du papyrus** (snow, embers, darkness) before their private mulligan; the AI chooses its own automatically. Sources swaps the two Mousses, two Gardiennes and two Poignes for two Porteuses, Eau de la source, another Festin, Ressac and Parade de verre; Le cercle replaces an Ashigaru, Brume and the Viking with Sahir des braises, Rempart de dunes and the Guerrier au bouclier rond. La source ardente replaces a Jarl and Anqa in Sources with La Lionne du midi ardent and Le Maître du marteau. Les serments du papyrus replaces both Mousses and Gardiennes in the base deck with two Guerriers and two Papyrus. All obey `validerDeckManaEssai`. Either deck may instead be customized in the local editor, which shows the printed effects and derives its colours from its cards. Both decks are independently validated against the 30-card, 1–3 colour and copy-limit rules and the MOBA audit gate before the match is created. Drafts last only while the board is open. The AI chooses its mana, discards, summons, targets, attacks, spells and blocks automatically. Its hand is never rendered; the human retains the supported response windows. The AI evaluates audited spell effects and up to 31 possible attack sets on isolated deterministic copies; it favors conquests and favorable trades but does not model hidden human responses. It remains a bounded heuristic, not an exhaustive strategy.

## Rules and engine integration

* `rpgMoba.js` owns serializable lanes: 2/1/2 slots per side, independent 10 HP values, assignments, active/conquered status and a winner after two conquests. Damage and healing are clamped; a conquered lane cannot be used again. Conquest immediately removes both sides' occupants via the engine's exile cleanup and stops further damage once a winner is decided. The original engine owns payment, attack readiness, combat modifiers, creature HP and spell effects.
* `rpgMobaLocal.js` uses an existing Mana Essai duel and maps its five slots to lanes. The engine's `pvp` flag selects manual control, not networking. It rejects occupied or inactive slots instead of allowing the classic full-board sacrifice and checks lane/engine placement agreement before game actions. Summons are rejected before mutation unless the creature belongs to the audited combat pool; this prevents an unsupported creature from entering play and deadlocking later combat.
* Creature blocks must be in the attacker's lane. An authoritative declaration applies banners once, then hands control to the defender. The defender can play audited instants before assigning blocks. Destroyed attackers leave the declaration; a surviving frozen attacker resolves under classic declared-combat rules. End of turn also opens a defender instant-response window, and only an explicit pass starts the next turn. The Lionne’s, Maître’s, Vipère’s, Sonneur’s and Corbeau’s creature-targeted arrivals additionally open the existing engine response window when the defender has a playable instant. Other response windows, including responses to targeted spells, are not yet exposed.
* Audited spells include the global trial spells, Poigne de givre and Ressac against an opposing creature, and Éclair du marteau / Trait de braise against a creature or selected active opposing lane. Ressac returns a creature to its owner’s hand and reconciles the lane and any declared attack. Parade de verre targets an allied creature and blocks its next hostile spell or effect according to the classic engine. Rempart de dunes grants its printed temporary defence bonus to allied creatures across active lanes. Papyrus pays its printed two-HP cost from a chosen friendly active lane with more than two HP, then draws using the original engine; it can also be played in supported response windows. Festin des vaillants heals all allied creatures and one chosen friendly lane; Eau de la source heals a chosen friendly lane. The Porteuse de cruches heals its own lane on arrival, including an audited scheduled return. The engine retains the card text and costs. Explicitly global creature effects remain global across active lanes.
* The five rapid ephemeral Braise creatures (Ifrit furtif and four Djinns) use printed Rapidité, Overkill, Envol and Infiltration where applicable, attack their own lane and are destroyed at end of turn. The engine journal and lane slots are reconciled after the transition. New audited spells include Tempête de sable, Vent rouge and Brisure de verre (hostile creatures across lanes), La Dernière Pesée (all creatures), Ressac de la lune noire (global return excluding Vampires), Rapport du greffier (draw), Les feuillets (opponent deck), Frappe d’iaijutsu, Courant de retour, Haut-fond, Lame de mer and Voile de la lune noire. The latter five require their printed friendly/enemy targets; the exiling Lame does not trigger Anqa’s death return. Classic instant timing, protection and shields still apply. The original five preset decks are unchanged; additions are available in the editor.
* The Veilleuse des citernes shields another chosen allied creature across lanes; with no legal ally, its existing engine fallback shields itself. Djinn de la flamme mourante draws on arrival, can attack immediately and expires at end of turn; Scalde des runes draws, while Valkyrie au corbeau draws only a Viking. Scribe des feuillets mills one enemy card on death, and Drakkar à tête de dragon cannot attack but draws on death. Conquest exiles without firing death effects. All six are available in the editor; starter decks are unchanged.
* Guerrier au bouclier rond enters its chosen lane and then requires its controller to select an allied creature (or itself) for its printed shield. Other actions are blocked while this target is pending; scheduled returns use the classic automatic arrival targeting.
* La Lionne du midi ardent targets an opposing creature when it arrives. If an instant response is available, the defender acts privately before the printed damage resolves; a removed target causes the effect to fizzle. Without a legal target, its arrival has no target.
* Le Maître du marteau can strike an opposing creature or its own opposing lane on arrival; the engine supplies its printed Furie, Overkill, Envol and Tenacité. A scheduled Tenacité return retains the original lane and routes an automatically selected sorcerer hit to that lane. If the return wins the game, later returns and turn-start effects are stopped.
* Sahir des braises draws on arrival and deals its printed pre-combat damage to the creature it blocks or that blocks it; it cannot strike a creature in another lane. Scheduled Anqa and Tenacité returns retain their originating lane, wait for a free slot there and are cancelled when that lane is conquered or unknown. The real Anqa global return effect is supported and board removals are reconciled after transitions. Other creation, exile and revival paths are not generally supported.
* By player decision, an empty deck produces no fatigue in MOBA. The agreed rule is: a creature effect targeting a sorcerer maps to the source creature's lane; a spell or HP cost without a creature source lets the player select an active lane; explicitly global effects remain global. This is implemented for the audited healing and spell-damage effects above; the Maître’s creature-source wizard damage and Papyrus’s HP cost are also routed; other HP-cost cards and most other wizard effects still require adaptation and remain unavailable. Classic fatigue and classic sorcerer HP/victory logic remain unchanged. MOBA victory is determined by lanes, not the engine's legacy wizard HP.

* La Dévoreuse exiles an opposing creature; Valkyrie des neuf nuits and Père des corbeaux exile an eligible creature from either camp under their printed cost threshold. Opponents can respond to hostile targets; allied targets resolve without an opposing response. Tenacité returns Père to his original lane and automatically repeats the arrival, as with the existing scheduled-return rule. Kraken can destroy an opposing Navire, with its normal response and death triggers.
* Appât de la meute and Embuscade mark blockers; each marked creature must block if a legal attacker is in its own lane. Attacks already targeted at creatures cannot be blocked, and the AI assigns forced blockers within their lanes. Temporary stat changes now remember their actual clamped deltas, so the 1/1 minimum does not turn into an unintended buff when they expire.
* Gardien de la porte ardente provides same-lane Défenseur priority, Provocation and targeted-attack retaliation. Rempart des sables éternels shields allies across lanes; Tin Hinan grants temporary Infiltration to other allies; Serpent du monde replaces both hands; Ifrit al-Ahmar grants global Djinn resonance; Serment de la forge grants temporary targeted attacks to allies.
* Yamina heals allied creatures globally and the friendly HP in her own lane; Dame des neuf mondes heals her own lane and has same-lane Attaque ciblée. Their creature-source healing never changes the legacy wizard HP.
* Chacal du seuil, Veilleuse au corbeau noir, Berserk du crépuscule, Seigneur des mâchoires, Faucon du zénith and Celui qui juge use Tribut after real damage to their own opposing lane; that lane's friendly HP is restored up to 10 while the opposing HP is reduced. A transfer can conquer a lane, but it cannot heal an already closed lane. Offrande de lin pays HP from a chosen friendly lane and grants temporary Tribut to allies across lanes. Prêtre de la bouche transfers on arrival, Ouvrier du natron transfers on death and Gardienne de la balance heals on death in their respective source lanes. Le Poids lets its caster select a lane for both sides of the transfer; La Grande Pesée separately selects a payment lane and transfer lane, honors La Dévoreuse's Lien, and draws unless that transfer wins immediately. Classic wizard HP and shields are not used in these MOBA transfers.
* Amani and Kenshi give their adjacency bonuses only to neighboring slots in the same lane: the touching edges of different lanes are not adjacent. Samurai resonance remains explicitly global. Rōnin du Soleil rouge, Hatamoto des cendres, Cavalier des deux sabres, Shogun promu, Onna-musha des cendres, Tengu du mont ardent, Kenshi and Susanoo keep their printed Harakiri: on death it chooses a living opposing creature across lanes, or strikes the opposing HP in the source's lane if no enemy creature exists. Shields and protection apply; conquest exiles without death effects, and a second conquest stops further triggers. Susanoo can exile either side's eligible creature costing five or more, with the normal hostile response window, and its Nuage de cendres synergy remains active.
* Le Maître des corbeaux uses the same neutral, cost-limited exile choice and hostile response window as Père des corbeaux, has lane-based Tribut, and returns to its original lane with Tenacité and an automatically chosen arrival target. Guetteur du beffroi counts its controller's turns; its final strike damages its own opposing lane, then draws unless the strike wins the match. Conquest removes its counters and prevents exiled neighbors from ringing. Its counters are visible on the MOBA board. Faussoyeur des dunes recycles the latest eligible graveyard creature on arrival and uses lane-based Butin; Dune marchante and Scribe de la Liste recover the latest eligible card on death (matching the classic engine's automatic recycling), but not when exiled by conquest.
* Tarek and Daimyō create dogs and samurai on arrival; Momie de lin and Orochi create Shabti and Petit Orochi on death. Tokens use the first free slot in their source's lane, then free slots in other active lanes in left-to-right order; any excess cannot enter. Simultaneous victims free their slots before their death triggers resolve. Tokens obey normal lane combat and permanent assignments, cannot be added to decks, and cease to exist rather than joining the MOBA graveyard on death or conquest. Tarek's aura remains lane-adjacent, Daimyō's banner applies to attacking samurai across lanes, and conquest causes no death triggers. This deterministic placement rule covers these audited tokens only.
* A creature's lane slot is reserved before its arrival resolves, allowing a summoned creature to die to chained death effects without rolling back a valid play.
* Fennec des décombres, Chien du guet and Le Renégat apply Butin only after real damage to their own opposing lane. A non-winning conquest still triggers the conquering attacker's Butin even though conquest exiles it; attacks skipped after that exile, prevented damage and attackers killed in ordinary combat do not trigger. The second conquest ends the game immediately, before further Butin choices. Multiple human loot discards are queued, while the AI discards automatically; an empty deck has no fatigue.
* Tiziri lets its controller select an eligible Magie Rapide or Sorcellerie from their deck; Pilote de la Carreira selects a Navire. The private human choice blocks other actions and the AI resolves its own search without rendering or logging its card. If no matching card exists, arrival finishes without a choice. Classic search behavior remains unchanged.
* Nom effacé and Celle qui pèse force the defender to discard; Ibis des comptes, Amayas, Jörmungandr and Le Routier draw then discard. When a human choice is required, the AI pauses even during its own turn and does not reveal its hand; the adapter blocks other actions until the discard is chosen.
* Attaque ciblée is restricted to opposing creatures in the attacker’s own lane, with same-lane Défenseur priority and the classic protection and miss rules. If there is no enemy creature there, the attacker may hit that lane’s HP; a target lost after declaration cancels the strike rather than redirecting it. The solo AI chooses targets in the same lane. The ten new targeted attackers include cards with secondary abilities, which still need broader MOBA-specific coverage.
* Völva soigneuse heals an allied creature (including herself), Faiseur de passage grants another ally temporary Infiltration, Vipère de verre debuffs an enemy, Sonneur fêlé returns an enemy and Corbeau de givre freezes an enemy costing at most three. The defender can respond to these hostile arrivals. Géant des banquises deals damage and freezes enemies; Léviathan returns other creatures across all lanes. Lane occupancy is reconciled after global arrivals.
* Effacement des faibles and Le Jugement des colosses target either side, enforcing the printed mana-cost threshold. Neige noire pays three HP from a chosen friendly lane before destroying cheap creatures and freezing survivors across the table. Nuage de cendres hits enemies across lanes and its echo resolves on the caster's next turn.

## Known limitations and risks

1. Most of the catalogue is unaudited: attacks, arrivals, deaths, auras, target selection, global effects, weapons, boarding, interception, tokens, HP costs, temporary exile and revival must all be checked before enabling arbitrary cards. Placement, combat and spell checks fail closed for unsupported creatures. The placement rollback for unexpected engine mutations restores serialized duel state, but not engine-side ephemeral journals or RNG; it is not a general transaction.
2. Only declaration, end-of-turn and the audited hostile creature-targeted arrival response windows are implemented. The classic engine's other fast-response, target and stack interactions need explicit lane-aware support. Some ability targeting choices and presentation also remain incomplete.
3. Real-browser desktop/mobile interaction, visual comparison against the classic duel, and accessibility testing remain necessary. Automated board tests run in jsdom with controlled fixtures, not a full browser. Chromium could not be launched here because system NSS/NSPR libraries were unavailable. There is no save/resume or online MOBA; online integration would additionally require authoritative lane validation, filtered views and concurrency/reconnect tests.
4. Further testing must cover the full catalogue, all entry/exit paths, forced/targeted combat, ongoing effects, post-victory actions and real-device complete matches. Do not describe this trial as the complete requested mode until those paths are implemented and verified.

## Files

* `src/games/rpgMoba.js` — lane bookkeeping, price payment and validation.
* `src/games/rpgMobaAI.js` — lane-aware heuristic decisions; probes use isolated copies and a separate deterministic RNG, while real moves use the adapter. It compares legal audited spells, simulates at most 31 attack sets and assigns blocks per lane using its own HP. It avoids gratuitous board wipes, late temporary buffs, and ephemeral summons after combat. It cannot predict hidden responses, and ignores milling as a win condition because MOBA has no fatigue.
* `src/games/rpgMobaLocal.js` — local adapter, audited deck validation and match creation, starter decks, turns, responses, audited spells, summons and combat.
* `src/games/rpgDuel.js` — optional lane damage/healing hooks, lane-aware blocking, conquest cleanup, scheduled returns, no-fatigue and lane victory handling. Ordinary classic calls retain their existing path.
* `src/games/RpgDuelPage.jsx` — local MOBA menu entry and shared classic card/mulligan/inspection and spell-announcement components; optional table-card props leave classic defaults unchanged. Online link remains intact.
* `src/games/RpgMobaBoard.jsx`, `src/games/RpgMobaDeckEditor.jsx`, `src/games/rpg-moba.css` — solo board, audited deck editor, automated AI turns and lane choices. The production board reuses the classic duel’s full-art table and mulligan cards, effect icons, cost/element/stat markers, card preview, compact spell announcement, card entry, surviving combat and spell-hit animations, and the original death ghosts (sand or Braise flames) on the affected slots; each seat’s terrain comes from the original deck-to-board resolver. The three-lane layout, HP and conquest UI remain MOBA-specific. Lane healing and blocked shields get feedback; the original SFX player handles selected spell, draw, healing and combat sounds and the existing mute preference, with a local sound toggle. Spell notifications capture the target before resolution. Animation settings are inherited from the classic game. Death ghosts are driven by the engine destruction journal, not returns to hand or conquest exiles; they never reserve a slot and are omitted in reduced motion while death sounds remain. Not all classic spell, death or audio effects are wired.
* `tests/rpg-moba.test.js`, `tests/rpg-moba-local.test.js`, `tests/rpg-moba-board.test.js`, `tests/rpg-mode-cards.test.js` — lane, adapter, jsdom board and menu coverage.

## Validation

* Focused MOBA, board, AI, mana, classic AI and shield tests: **349/349 passed**; PvP: **53/53 passed**. Classic duel, life transfer, adjacency and related card regressions: **415/415 passed**. Build, `git diff --check` and the classic animation-settings script passed. Earlier card-pool and adapter tests also covered sixteen deterministic games with edited decks.
* Forty-five deterministic AI-versus-AI matches using preset and customized decks reach victory with legal lane combat and supported instant responses. Additional tests cover spell/conquest priorities, board wipes, suicide attacks, newly audited cards, human response windows, hidden AI hands and automatic turns in jsdom. The AI is heuristic, not a full strategic search.
* Full RPG suite: **1,537/1,549 passed**, with the same twelve previously reproduced baseline failures (catalogue and Link expectations, route wrapping, image sizes and Souk offer count).
* Real-browser desktop/mobile interaction and accessibility still need verification; Chromium could not be launched here because system NSS/NSPR libraries were unavailable.
