SEGA and Atlus have revealed the first proper gameplay of Persona 6, closing months of silence around the next chapter of the JRPG series. The eight-minute footage confirms a Tokyo setting and a release window now set for late 2027.

The trailer shows the protagonist navigating a Shibuya-inspired district by day, before dungeons open at night through doors scattered across the city. Atlus calls this system « Gates », replacing the Towers of Persona 5.

The party system has also been reworked: up to five members can fight simultaneously, with combo commands triggered between two characters sharing a social bond. A first for the series, according to the developer.

Persona 6 is confirmed for Nintendo Switch 2, PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PC, with a physical edition planned on Switch 2. SEGA has not announced a price or a precise date, but the publisher describes late 2027 as a « firm window ».
